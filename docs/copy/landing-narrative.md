## Section: how it works (three beats)

1. **We state a claim.** A probability, a country, a threshold, a date.
   Written to the book before the window opens; never edited after.
2. **The world answers.** OONI's measurements or the FX reference rate —
   sources we don't control — settle every call on schedule, unattended.
3. **The score publishes either way.** Brier per kind, skill only when three
   independent batches exist. A withheld number states its reason. The
   misses stay on the page.

## Section: the honest bit (pull quote)

Our first cohort settled on 5 September 2026: ten hits, twenty-four misses,
five held for thin evidence — and the sharpest finding was about our own
instrument. We published that, too. [→ the instrument essay]

## Section: the brief

One email a day. The record first, then the board. Written by a model,
gated by rules that refuse ungrounded numbers, with a deterministic
edition when the model's draft doesn't clear the gate — and it says so.

## Footer line

Free. No accounts, no keys, no tiers. Every number auditable, every rule
published before it applied.
